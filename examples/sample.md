# Episode 1: Why your first video flops

Prep notes, not read aloud: B-roll list is in the shot doc. Keep the intro under 20 seconds.

---

**[Hook]**

You spent a whole weekend on your first video. Twelve people watched it. Three of them were your family.

Today I show you the four mistakes that kill a first video before anyone presses play. And none of them is about your camera.

**[Mistake 1: the slow start]**

Most first videos open with "Hi everyone, welcome back to my channel." Nobody came back. It is your first video.

You have about three seconds before the thumb moves on. Use them to say what the viewer gets, not who you are.

**[Mistake 2: no promise]**

A title is a promise. "My trip to Lisbon" promises nothing. "I spent 300 euros in Lisbon and this is what it bought" promises a number, a place and a story.

**[Outro]**

Fix these, and your second video already beats your first. If you want the checklist, the link is in the description.
